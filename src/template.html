    Advanced: Variables

    Variables are four arrows that come from the finest detail of your Sun and Moon nodes. Jovian Archive teaches them only after you have lived by your Strategy and Authority for a while, and they need a birth time that is exact to the minute.

      Planet positions match Swiss Ephemeris, the reference most Human Design software uses (based on NASA JPL data), to within one arcsecond: in a test of 10,000 random births, 2 charts differed by one line. The chart logic is checked against independent Human Design programs and against published charts. A birth within a minute of a line boundary can still differ between programs, so an exact birth time matters. Addresses come from OpenStreetMap (Photon), towns from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0); the time zone follows from the exact location.
